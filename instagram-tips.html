Légende

Légende

Overlay

Légende
freemium works when: → the free tier lets users experience the core value → the upgrade is an obvious "I want more of this" → the marginal cost of a free user is very low freemium doesn't work when: → the free tier is so good no one upgrades → the free tier is so limited users never see the real value → the product requires significant setup before value kicks in the question: does your free tier make the paid tier feel obvious? if yes: freemium is your best acquisition tool if no: a trial with a time limit usually converts better save this 🔖

Overlay

Légende

Overlay
annual vs monthly pricing: the math that makes annual worth it.
Légende
monthly subscription: flexible, lower commitment, higher churn annual: upfront cash, lower churn, user is invested the math: a user who pays $10/month churns after 3 months = $30 LTV a user who pays $80/year (33% discount) = $80 LTV, guaranteed even with the discount, annual is almost always better economics the trick: make annual feel like a good deal, not a lock-in "save 33%" works better than "pay upfront for a year" framing matters as much as the price save this 🔖

Légende

Légende
thin pages compete with each other for the same query they split your authority instead of concentrating it Google has to choose between them and often ranks none well the fix: consolidate near-duplicate content into one authoritative page redirect the others with 301s fewer, better pages > more, weaker pages the crawl budget is real: Google only crawls so many pages on your site don't waste it on pages that shouldn't exist quality over quantity. every time. save this 🔖

Blockchain & Web3

Overlay
what gas fees actually are (and why they fluctuate).
Légende
every transaction on Ethereum needs to be processed by the network validators who process transactions are paid in ETH → that's the gas fee why they fluctuate: it's a bidding system when lots of people want their transactions processed quickly, fees go up when the network is quiet, fees are low this is why you'll sometimes pay $3 to send $5 layer 2s (Arbitrum, Optimism, Base) solve this: they batch transactions off the main chain and settle them together → fees drop dramatically save this if you're new to crypto 🔖
Overlay
why crypto wallets don't store your crypto.
Légende
your crypto lives on the blockchain, not in your wallet a wallet is just a key → public key = your address (like an email, you can share it) → private key = your password (never share this) the wallet stores the private key the private key lets you sign transactions signed transactions move funds on the blockchain lose the private key → lose access forever someone else gets the private key → they own your funds "not your keys, not your coins" means: if your funds are on an exchange, the exchange holds the keys, not you save this 🔖
Overlay
DeFi explained without the hype.
Légende
DeFi = Decentralised Finance traditional finance: a bank holds your money, processes your transactions, decides your interest rate DeFi: smart contracts do all of this, with rules that are public and can't be changed by anyone unilaterally things you can do with DeFi: → lend your crypto and earn interest → borrow against your holdings without a credit check → trade tokens without a centralised exchange the real risks: → smart contract bugs (irreversible, often uninsurable) → liquidation if collateral value drops → rug pulls (founders abandon the project) the technology is real. the risks are also real. save this 🔖
Overlay
NFTs: what the technology actually does, separate from the speculation.
Légende
NFT = Non-Fungible Token fungible = interchangeable (one bitcoin = one bitcoin) non-fungible = unique (this specific token ≠ any other token) what the tech does: proves ownership of a unique digital item on a public blockchain anyone can verify who owns it, and the history of every transfer the speculation around JPEGs was separate from the underlying technology actual useful applications: → event tickets that can't be counterfeited → digital credentials and certificates → in-game items with verified scarcity → royalties that pay creators automatically on resale the hype was overblown. the technology has real applications. save this 🔖
